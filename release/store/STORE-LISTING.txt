APP NAME
Sentinel

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Monitor your Linux PC over SSH: health, processes, tasks and a basic terminal.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Check your Linux PC from your Android phone using your existing SSH connection.
Sentinel shows system responsiveness, CPU and memory use, storage, network/disk
rates, available sensors and processes. Sort processes by CPU or RAM and filter
by local user. Arrange widgets in editable tabs and choose Nord, Monochrome or
Amber themes. Sensor availability and process visibility depend on your PC.

Watch selected processes using boot/PID/start identity. A process disappearing
means its outcome is unknown; it does not prove success. An optional command
runner on the PC records the direct command's exit result. Expected-success
rules check recorded exits; they do not schedule work or verify backup contents.

Start an optional background monitoring session with an ongoing notification
and Stop control. Choose notification severity, quiet hours, cooldowns, task
mute and sustained health warnings. Checks and notification delivery are best
effort: Android, battery saving and network loss may delay them. App/process stop
or phone restart ends the session; restart it in Sentinel. There is no cloud relay.

Optional terminal access provides a basic SSH shell using a separate authorised
key. It does not mirror Kitty or provide remote desktop. Monitoring retains its
restricted read-only key. Saved PC/login profiles and macro templates are
encrypted locally; phone verification protects saved connection settings and
terminal details. A secure phone screen lock is required for those features.

Shutdown and reboot require a held arming control and the PC account's sudo
password, entered fresh for each request and never saved. No fingerprint or
phone PIN is required for power actions. Existing sudo rights, systemd and the
Terminal SSH key's existing authorisation are required. Blocking inhibitors are
respected; there is no forced action or automatic retry. No additional PC
software or permission change is made for power actions.

Your connection goes directly to the configured PC. Sentinel has no cloud
accounts, ads, analytics or developer telemetry. Connection settings, process
watches and their bounded history remain encrypted on the phone. Live readings
and terminal scrollback stay in memory. Optional runner results and expected-
success rules remain on the PC; see the privacy policy for retention/deletion.

Requirements: Android 10+, a reachable Linux PC with SSH and Python 3, and
administrator-reviewed helper installation and public-key authorisation. Use
an existing LAN or VPN route such as Tailscale; Sentinel does not configure a
VPN, install a PC service or keep the PC awake. Updated helper capabilities are
needed for newer metrics and tasks. Directory-service accounts may be absent.
Windows/macOS hosts and guaranteed unattended monitoring are unsupported.
An unreachable PC may be asleep, off or disconnected; Sentinel cannot prove which.
